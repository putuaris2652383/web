## Overview

Led the Carbon Vlogger Workshop & Challenge with MyCarbonSteps x Carbon Academy as a national virtual climate education project. The program combined climate education with digital storytelling to make carbon-footprint awareness more actionable for young people.

## Scale & Achievements

- 35 participants from multiple provinces across Indonesia.
- Secured **IDR 2.5 million** in sponsor funding.
- 13 environmental vlogs produced and promoted on Instagram.
- 77% of participants completed both pre- and post-tests.
- Average knowledge increase of **14.7%** in carbon-footprint understanding, with an N-Gain score of **0.38**.
- 20,000+ total vlog views and 30,000+ impressions.

## My Contributions

- Designed the training flow and educational content.
- Developed pre- and post-test evaluation methods and qualitative assessment.
- Created the social-media dissemination strategy.
- Integrated green-jobs and climate-storytelling themes.
- Coordinated project timeline, deliverables, reporting, stakeholders, and digital campaign outputs.

## Model

The project used a hybrid climate-education and digital-storytelling model based on the AIDA framework: Attention, Interest, Desire, and Action. The approach was designed as a low-cost, replicable model for communities and local governments.
