## Overview

Developed during the Master of Applied Climatology at IPB University, this project used 30 years of simulated weather data from four stations (1995–2024) to predict surface temperature with a FeedForward Neural Network (FNN).

## Approach

- Seasonal pattern identification, normalization, and lag-feature generation.
- FNN model building using Keras with hyperparameter tuning using Keras Tuner.
- Cross-variable analysis of temperature, rainfall, humidity, and solar radiation.
- Evaluation using Pearson correlation, Taylor diagram, MAE, and RMSE.

## Key Results

- Pearson correlation of **R = 0.97**.
- Clear seasonal trends and climatic shifts identified through moving averages.
- Demonstrated the effectiveness of ANN for nonlinear climate systems.
- Model supports decision-making in agriculture, disaster preparedness, and local adaptation.

## Tools

Python, Keras, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, climate data analytics, neural network tuning, and time-series analysis.
