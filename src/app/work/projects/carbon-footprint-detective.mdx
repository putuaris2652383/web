## Overview

I developed this interactive tool to make carbon-footprint concepts more tangible. Instead of presenting emissions as abstract numbers, the experience guides users through a series of practical cases—organic waste, biowash, plastics, mobility and energy—then turns the results into a shareable investigation report.

## What it demonstrates

- Activity-based CO₂e estimation with explicit emission-factor assumptions.
- A guided, game-like learning flow for climate literacy.
- Hypothesis-versus-actual comparison for reflective learning.
- A generated report that can be shared after completing the investigation.
- A prepared cloud-submission integration point for future data collection.

## Methodology

The calculator documents its assumptions directly in the interface, including the use of US EPA emission-factor references and WARM-style waste calculations. For Indonesian deployment, the displayed factors should be reviewed and localized against the most appropriate Indonesian datasets before being used for formal inventory or reporting.
